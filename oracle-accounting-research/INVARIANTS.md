# Executable invariants and measurement plan

These are differential properties derived from the incident corpus. `PASS` in a local model is not a mainnet safety claim. Every economic test must record token units, block/pin, signed attacker/protocol/victim deltas, and costs.

| ID | Unit-aware invariant | Function/boundary | Executable measurement and negative control | Incident references |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OA-01 | For cap `C` in collateral units, every path that increases represented supplier claims must enforce `representedAfter <= C` or an explicit risk transition. | Compound cToken mint, direct transfer, third-party credit, exchange-rate update | Mint below cap; attempt over-cap mint; donate raw token; compare `shares(user)*cash/totalSupply`. Control: internal cash excludes direct gifts. | MAMO, Venus THE |
| OA-02 | A direct donation must not create attacker net value merely by increasing `balanceOf`; `PnL = withdrawal - ownDeposit - donationCost - fees - gas`. | ERC4626 `deposit/mint/redeem`, lending cash, adapter residual | Pre-seed victim and attacker separately. Self-donation-only profit is rejected. Require a non-donor victim or residual owned by someone else. | Lodestar, wUSDM, Raft |
| OA-03 | At `totalSupply ∈ {0,1 wei}`, a deposit that mints zero shares must revert or transfer exactly zero assets; no victim assets may be burned without shares. | First deposit, `previewDeposit`, rounding floor/ceil | Test zero supply, one-wei supply, donation before first mint, repeated 1-wei deposits versus one large deposit. | Resupply, Hundred, Sonne, Radiant, Raft |
| OA-04 | Any collateral/liquidation PPS change must satisfy configured upward and downward bound `L <= PPS_after/PPS_before <= U` over the chosen time window. | `convertToAssets`, `pricePerShare`, `totalAssets/totalSupply`, AUM/share | Donate at 1 wei and capital tiers; compose with AMM movement. Control: cap/smooth every multiplicand, not only the pool EMA. | sDOLA, Cream, Inverse, Edel, Makina |
| OA-05 | `depositCap=0` must not be treated as strategy removal. An impaired strategy must contribute zero unreviewed NAV or have bounded conservative valuation before shares can mint/redeem. | Active strategy set, `getActiveArks`, `totalAssets`, offboarding | Set cap zero without removal; externally credit stale receipt; redeem pre-acquired shares. Control: remove strategy and repeat. | Silo soUSDC, Lazy Summer |
| OA-06 | Redemption must identify the liquidity source. `healthyLiquidDelta <= 0` must not fund payout against a non-withdrawable inflated constituent beyond its realizable value. | Withdrawal queue, multi-strategy ERC4626 | Log per-strategy assets and liquid balances before/after. Assert which healthy market pays and the residual victim claim. | Silo, Summer |
| OA-07 | Invalid signature/proof/signer/verifier must revert before storing a price or releasing value. | Oracle verifier, DVN, bridge message consumer | Submit `(validData,falseProof)`, valid proof from wrong signer, and valid paired control. Check stored value and balances remain zero on failure. | Bonzo, Kelp |
| OA-08 | A price is valid only if feed identity, round freshness, min/max, and consumer pause conditions all pass; `answer > 0` alone is insufficient. | `latestRoundData`, pull-oracle verification, stale cache | Stale timestamp, future timestamp, extreme answer, feed floor above market, deprecated feed. Consumer must fail closed for collateral issuance/liquidation. | LUNA, Ostium, Balance, Full Sail |
| OA-09 | For composed quotes, `asset/base × base/quote` must include every conversion exactly once with matching decimals and direction. | Oracle configuration and adapters | Wrong-feed BTC/USD-for-USDC, omitted ETH/USD, 8/18 decimals, inverted rate. Compare against independent unit computation. | cbETH, Ploutos, Ribbon |
| OA-10 | Interdependent configuration must update atomically: `(snapshotRatio,snapshotTimestamp)` refers to the same observation. | CAPO/risk steward, oracle upgrades | Partial ratio update plus advanced timestamp must revert. Check both upward and downward effective-price errors and liquidation pause. | Aave CAPO |
| OA-11 | Manipulation cost plus delay must exceed extractable value; a median/pivot is valid only while constituent independence and depth hold. | Spot/TWAP/median oracle and borrow/liquidation engine | Sweep capital, pool depth, source count, update delay, and guard threshold. Include sustained multi-venue convergence, not only one-block rejection. | bZx, Harvest, UwU, Venus THE, Tectonic |
| OA-12 | Route credit must equal actual terminal balance delta. Intermediate outputs reconsumed by later hops contribute exactly zero to final credit. | Margin `verify_token_out`, aggregator adapters, callbacks | Circular `A→B→A→B`: naive terminal sum 180 versus actual 80; reject nonfinal terminal production/reconsumption. Recheck actual received amount at callback. | Rhea |
| OA-13 | `path[0] == declaredInput` and `path[last] == declaredOutput`; path length and every hop must be canonical and connected. | `Looping._swap`, leverage open/close, reward swapper | Correct endpoints execute against faithful pool; wrong terminal cannot supply declared asset or affect a victim. Check first-token residual/allowance separately. | Rhea, Ionic; HyperLend recheck |
| OA-14 | Collateral address must equal canonical issuer/deployment and provenance; notional mint/supply is not realizable value. | Listing, mint authority, bridge-wrapped asset | Counterfeit token with genuine-looking pool/feed must be rejected. Record nominal mint separately from actual downstream withdrawal. | Ionic, Echo, Drift |
| OA-15 | A finding requires `attackerDelta > 0` in liquid asset after principal, donation, flash-loan fee, slippage, collateral loss and gas; protocol bad debt alone is insufficient. | Every PoC and incident comparison | Print signed attacker, protocol, victim and residual balances. Track gross borrow, attacker net, bad debt, frozen/returned/rolled-back value separately. | Raft, sDOLA, Venus THE, Tectonic |
| OA-16 | Issuer-affiliate tokens and receipt assets receive the same oracle, PPS, donation, route and withdrawal-source checks as external assets. | FRAX/FXB and related vaults, protocol-issued stablecoins | Do not exempt an internal oracle or 1:1 assumption. Test depeg, stale rate, actual redemption liquidity, and external credit paths. | Resupply, Silo; Frax-CVX recheck |
| OA-17 | Read-only state must be consistent across external calls; no price/collateral read may observe a partially updated vault/pool state. | Curve `remove_liquidity`, callbacks, oracle getters | Reenter getter during withdrawal and compare with pre/post state. A view function is not automatically reentrancy-safe. | dForce, Cream |
| OA-18 | Post-action solvency must be evaluated on actual resulting debt/collateral, including self-liquidation and donation states. | Leverage open, liquidation, donation/repay | Attempt position creation at exact threshold and one wei beyond. Control with post-action health check; do not blame an external feed without evidence. | Euler, Rhea |

## Foundry counterexamples currently implemented

`poc/OracleAccountingBoundaries.t.sol` contains seven passing tests covering OA-01, OA-05/06, OA-08/11, OA-12/13, and OA-07:

- raw-cash represented claim `180 > cap 100`, with internal-cash control at 80;
- cap-zero active NAV redemption: input 100, stale cost explicitly zero, payout 145, pre-gas P&L +45, honest liquid residual 55; removal control payout 100;
- circular route naive output 180 rejected, direct terminal output 80 accepted;
- unsigned/wrong-signer submissions rejected only by guarded consumer;
- 180 price rejected at ±5% bound while 104 passes; unrelated EMA does not cap 13.79% PPS jump;
- actual `Looping` correct route creates 100 supply/100 debt for caller only; wrong terminal reverts under faithful 1:1 health-check mock, with zero victim deltas.

Log: `oracle-accounting-research/boundary-tests.log`. These models do not reproduce any 2026 incident and do not establish current-mainnet impact.
