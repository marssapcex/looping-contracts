Promotion requires a permissionless call path, a pinned-BSC withdrawal, and positive attacker P&L after all costs. No candidate meets that gate. `LOCAL_LEAD` means a real local EVM counterexample exists but mainnet impact is unproven; `REJECTED` means code/test analysis removes the claimed money-loss path or the path is explicitly out of scope.

## Mechanism-matched audit comparison

The comparison uses the two exact supplied audit PDFs, not an absent incident corpus:

- Hacken's v1 duplicate-LP and malicious-token findings rely on owner adding assets; its oracle concern likewise concerns owner configuration. Those paths are excluded rather than re-reported as permissionless bugs.
- PeckShield's V2 `LPStaking.emergencyWithdraw` omitted `adjustedSupply` adjustment. The supplied v1 contract is different and does reduce `pool.allocPoint` and `totalAllocPoint` at `contracts/LpTokenStaker.sol:274-284`; the V2 issue is not copied here.
- Neither prior report proves the stale-schedule path on deployed BSC state. LEAD-001 comes from this audit's exact deployed-source PoC, not from a historical incident or loss figure.

`vulnchain` was not present. No incident ID or loss amount is invented.
