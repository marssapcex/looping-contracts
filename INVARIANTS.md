## Explicitly non-applicable generic invariants

The requested share/redemption, AMM `k`, tick, first-depositor, and oracle-round checks do not map to any implementation in this checkout: there is no vault share mint/redeem, AMM reserve/tick accounting, or Chainlink round reader. They are not silently marked “passed.” The external lending pool and wHLP depositor require their own source and a pinned fork for those checks. The local campaign substitutes balance, route freshness, callback authentication, and per-user debt/collateral conservation invariants above.
